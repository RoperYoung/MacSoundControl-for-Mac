# NeoSoundControl 1.0.13

Mac Sound Control 现已更名为 NeoSoundControl。新版继续使用已有的音量和偏好设置。

- 菜单栏面板宽度调整为 342 pt，文字和设备行比 1.0.12 略大。
- 在偏好设置的“外观”卡片中切换跟随系统、浅色或深色，选择简体中文或 English，或让语言跟随系统。
- “状态栏显示图标”开关立即控制右上角的扬声器图标。隐藏后仍可在主窗口打开菜单栏控制。
- 页脚的访问提示与顶部卡片左边缘对齐。

## English

Mac Sound Control is now NeoSoundControl. The app keeps your existing volumes and preferences.

- The menu bar panel is now 342 pt wide, with slightly larger text and device rows.
- The Appearance card in Preferences lets you follow the system theme or select Light or Dark. Choose Simplified Chinese, English, or the system language.
- Show Icon in Menu Bar controls the speaker icon. When it is hidden, you can still open the controls from the main window.
- The footer hint aligns with the left edge of the cards.
