NeoSoundControl 隐私政策

本政策适用于 Mac App Store 版 NeoSoundControl，更新于 2026 年 11 月 24 日。

声音与设置

应用直接在你的 Mac 上处理麦克风和正在播放声音的应用。音频缓冲仅用于实时电平检测和音量调整，处理完毕即丢弃。开发者不接收音频、设备列表、使用记录或偏好设置。

界面和各应用音量偏好保存在本机。应用无需账号，不含广告、跟踪或遥测。可在系统设置的「隐私与安全性」中撤销麦克风或系统音频权限。移除应用容器可清除其本地设置。

联系开发者

如有隐私问题，请通过技术支持联系。主动打开外部网页时，该网站自己的隐私政策适用。

NeoSoundControl Privacy Policy

This policy covers the Mac App Store edition of NeoSoundControl. Updated November 24, 2026.

Audio and preferences

Audio from the microphone and from playing apps is processed on your Mac. Buffers are discarded after live level measurement or volume adjustment. The developer receives no audio, device lists, usage records or preferences.

Interface preferences and per-app levels are stored locally. The app has no account system, advertising, tracking or telemetry. You can revoke microphone and system audio access in System Settings under Privacy & Security. Removing the app container clears its local preferences.

Contact

For privacy questions, visit Support. External websites you choose to open are covered by their own privacy policies.
