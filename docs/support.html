NeoSoundControl 技术支持

打开主窗口或点击菜单栏的扬声器，即可切换输入输出设备和调整声音。

常见问题

按应用调整音量需要 macOS 15 或更新版本。播放声音后在「应用音量」中选择「分开控制」，根据系统提示允许系统音频访问。

测试麦克风时，在「声音测试」点击开始，并在 macOS 的「隐私与安全性」中允许麦克风访问。部分 HDMI 或 DisplayPort 设备只能用显示器或音响上的按键调节音量。

状态栏图标可以在「偏好设置 → 外观 → 状态栏显示图标」中恢复。

NeoSoundControl Support

Open the main window or click the speaker in the menu bar to switch devices and adjust volume.

Troubleshooting

Per-app volume requires macOS 15 or later. Play audio, select Separate Control and allow system audio access when prompted. For microphone testing, start Sound Test and allow microphone access in Privacy & Security. Some HDMI or DisplayPort outputs require volume adjustment on the display or receiver.

Restore the menu bar icon in Preferences under Appearance. For help, describe the issue, macOS version and steps to reproduce. Reports are public. Contact the developer on GitHub.
